How to use the app

This example shows how a customer, seller, and dashboard user can fill in the Sela Volts system.

Go to Customer portal Go to Seller portal Go to Dashboard

1. Customer example

  1. Open the Customer portal.
  2. Fill in the form:
Example values:
Full name: Jane Uwase
Phone number: 0788123456
Plate number: RAA 123 A
Vehicle type: Car

Then click “Register vehicle”.

2. Seller example

  1. Open the Seller portal.
  2. Fill in:
Example values:
Vehicle ID: 1713456789000
Charger: CHARGER-01
Amount due: 5000
Seller name: Seller 1
Phone number: 0788123456
Provider: MTN MoMo

Check the confirmation box, click “Start charging”, then request and confirm payment.

3. Dashboard example

Open the Dashboard to view:

Example data:
Customers: 12
Vehicles: 12
Sessions: 18
Revenue: 150000 RWF

The table should show recent charging sessions and payment status.

Full sample scenario

  1. A customer registers plate number RAA 123 A.
  2. A seller starts a charge session.
  3. The seller requests payment by mobile money.
  4. The payment is confirmed.
  5. The dashboard updates with the new revenue and session record.
